DIEU KHOAN SU DUNG - ic3_collect_points va TaoDeThi
===================================================

Phan mem nay do Nguyen Thuan An (sau day goi la "Tac gia") cap phep cho tung nguoi dung ca nhan.
Bang viec mo va su dung phan mem, ban dong y voi cac dieu khoan sau.

1. Quyen duoc cap. Ban duoc dung phan mem tren cac may ghi trong file account.lic cua ban, trong thoi han ghi trong file do,
   de thu diem bai thi o lop hoc cua ban. Quyen nay khong chuyen nhuong va khong doc quyen.
2. Khong duoc: sao chep de phat cho nguoi khac; dua file account.lic hoac mat khau cho nguoi khac; dich nguoc, giai ma,
   thao go, trich xuat ma nguon hay khoa cua phan mem; go bo, lam sai lech co che giay phep hoac dong ho; ban lai, cho thue
   hoac dang tai phan mem len noi cong khai.
3. De thi. Phan mem chi xu ly cac file de thi do ban co quyen su dung. Ban chiu trach nhiem ve quyen su dung cac de thi do.
4. Du lieu. Du lieu (truong, lop, diem) nam tren may cua ban, Tac gia khong thu thap va khong gui di dau. Ban tu sao luu.
5. Het han va thu hoi. Giay phep tu het han vao ngay ghi trong account.lic. Tac gia co the khong cap moi neu ban vi pham dieu khoan.
6. Khong bao dam. Phan mem duoc cung cap "nhu hien co", khong co bao dam nao ve tinh phu hop hay hoat dong khong loi.
   Tac gia khong chiu trach nhiem cho thiet hai phat sinh tu viec su dung, bao gom mat diem do loi may, mang hoac de thi.
7. Ma nguon va ban quyen thuoc ve Tac gia. Cac thu vien ma nguon mo di kem van theo giay phep cua tung thu vien.
