ic3_collect_points - Thu diem bai thi trong phong may (khong can Internet)
==========================================================================

CAU TRUC THU MUC
----------------
Ngoai cung chi co 2 file de dung: ic3_collect_points.exe (phan mem chinh) va TaoDeThi.exe (tao de cho hoc sinh).
Thu muc he_thong chua cac file phu (khong can mo): giao dien, scripts (lay_ip.bat...), Data (du lieu), account.lic.

A. LAN DAU TIEN (tren may giao vien)
------------------------------------
1. Giai nen thu muc nay o noi ban co quyen ghi (vi du D:\ic3_collect_points). Khong chay truc tiep trong file .zip.
2. Mo ic3_collect_points.exe. Cua so den hien ra: DUNG DONG no khi dang dung phan mem.
   Trinh duyet tu mo trang dang nhap. Neu Windows hoi tuong lua (Firewall), chon "Cho phep" tren mang rieng.
3. Man hinh dang nhap hien "Ma may". Chep ma do gui cho tac gia de duoc cap file account.lic
   (tac gia gui kem ten dang nhap va mat khau rieng). Mot so tai khoan dac biet khong can ma may.
4. Nhan file account.lic: bam "Chon file account.lic" tren man hinh dang nhap (hoac chep file vao thu muc he_thong). Roi dang nhap bang ten dang nhap (hoac email) va mat khau tac gia gui rieng.
5. Tai khoan co the khong rang buoc may (dung moi may) hoac chi chay tren cac may da ghi trong file (thuong 4).
   Muon them may o loai thu hai: gui ma may moi cho tac gia, nhan file moi.
6. Tai khoan co han (thuong 4 thang). Het han thi khong dang nhap va hoc sinh khong gui duoc diem; xin tac gia file moi.

B. TAO DE CHO HOC SINH (TaoDeThi.exe)
-------------------------------------
1. Keo file de thi .exe (vi du GS6_LV1_TESTING_01.exe) tha len bieu tuong TaoDeThi.exe. Co the keo nhieu file mot luc.
2. Canh file goc se co them file ..._LAN.exe. Day la file phat cho hoc sinh. File goc khong bi sua.
3. File _LAN.exe co the dung lai nhieu lan, khong can tao lai moi buoi.

C. MOI BUOI THI
---------------
1. Tren may giao vien: mo ic3_collect_points.exe, dang nhap, chon truong -> lop -> Dashboard.
2. Tao file lay_ip.bat: o man hinh Dashboard, vao Cong cu, bam tao lay_ip.bat (tu dien dung IP may giao vien hien tai).
   File nam trong he_thong\scripts\. May giao vien la laptop nen IP co the doi theo mang: tao lai moi noi moi buoi.
3. Chep lay_ip.bat va cac file _LAN.exe sang may hoc sinh (USB hoac thu muc chia se trong mang LAN).
4. Tren moi may hoc sinh: bam doi lay_ip.bat MOT lan truoc gio thi (mat vai giay, file tu bien mat), roi mo file _LAN.exe lam bai.
5. Hoc sinh nop bai: diem tu gui ve may giao vien va hien o Dashboard. Hoc sinh khong mo duoc giao dien cua giao vien.

D. XU LY SU CO
--------------
- May hoc sinh bao "Khong gui duoc IP": kiem tra cung mang, tat tuong lua may giao vien tam thoi hoac
  chay he_thong\scripts\fix_firewall.bat bang quyen quan tri tren may giao vien, roi chay lai lay_ip.bat.
- Diem khong ve: giao vien phai dang mo phan mem va giay phep con han. Bai da nop van nam tren may hoc sinh va se tu gui lai.
- "Dong ho may bi chinh lui": dat lai ngay gio dung roi mo lai phan mem.
- Sai mat khau nhieu lan se bi khoa tam thoi (toi da 15 phut).

E. DU LIEU
----------
Moi du lieu (truong, lop, diem) nam trong he_thong\Data\. Sao luu bang cach chep thu muc Data nay.
